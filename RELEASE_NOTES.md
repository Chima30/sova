## Install SOVA on Windows

1. Download **SOVA-Setup-0.5.0.exe** below.
2. Run it. Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer is not code-signed yet: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose where to install, then open SOVA from the desktop or Start menu shortcut.
4. Accept the camera consent, then paste your free Presage API key in **Settings** (get one at https://physiology.presagetech.com/auth/register).
5. Allow desktop apps to use the camera: Windows Settings › Privacy & security › Camera › **Let desktop apps access your camera**.

Requires Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, and a webcam. Live readings need an internet connection.

## What's in this version

New in 0.5.0:

- A new look: frosted-glass panels, a side icon rail, and orange reserved for what SOVA really measures, with lavender for guidance and grey when nothing is measured
- A 3D heart in the focus session that swells with your breath and beats at your measured pulse, only while each reading is usable
- A minute after a breathing reset, SOVA shows your breathing, pulse and HRV before and after, side by side
- Smoother motion: cards rise in, charts draw in, and changed readings slide in. All of it stops with reduced motion
- 60-second signal check-in from Home: breathing, pulse and HRV with live signal quality, compared with your last check-in and your own usual range, saved separately from focus sessions
- Light and dark mode, following Windows by default, with a switch in the top bar and in Settings
- A local profile: SOVA asks your name and what brings you here, then greets you on Home. No account, no password, stored only on your computer
- "How are you feeling?" check-in on Home, saved with the session and summarised in Insights
- Quick resets for common moments: overwhelmed, before something stressful, between meetings, winding down, tired eyes
- New breathing patterns: 4-7-8, Coherent 5-5 and Triangle 4-4-4, with coaching that follows each pattern
- Reset length from 30 seconds to 10 minutes

New in 0.4.0: a full redesign.

- Breath lines: a ribbon of contour lines that follows your real breathing in teal, the breathing guide in lavender, and stays still and grey when nothing is being measured
- New workstation layout: timer ring, breathing first, pulse and HRV as supporting readings, a live session timeline
- Top navigation, a session pill to jump back to a running session from any section
- Redesigned Home, Focus, Recovery, Insights, History, Sounds and Settings, with a privacy dashboard
- New sound option: keep playing during breaks
- Tooltips for every biometric term, honest empty states, reduced-motion support throughout

Everything from earlier versions:

- Live breathing, pulse, HRV, blinking and talking from the Presage SmartSpectra SDK, compared with your personal baseline
- Focus presets, adaptive breaks, tension cues, eye-rest reminders, meeting snooze and away pause
- Breathing resets with live biofeedback, plus optional microphone breath analysis
- Floating focus widget, quick reset shortcut (Ctrl + Shift + B), ambient sounds
- Insights: timeline replay, before-and-after, weekly reflection, personal patterns
- Optional Presage AI reflections
- Everything stored only on your computer, with export and delete all

See the README for full consent and data handling details.
